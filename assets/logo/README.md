Logo files. The SVGs are vector redraws made from a screenshot of the first logo design, so keep the Canva original as the artwork of record and swap in its SVG export when available; the filenames can stay the same.

- `logo-lockup.svg`: mark, "sakura" and NAILS all in `sakura-red`, on transparent. Use on `cream`, white and `blush`.
- `logo-lockup-reversed.svg`: all `cream` artwork on a `sakura-red` background.
- `logo-lockup-mono-dark.svg`: single ink, `ink` (#2B1B1A), for one-colour printing and stamps.
- `logo-mark.svg`: the five-petal mark alone in `sakura-red`. Each petal is a nail: a solid nail bed inside a closed outline (60% opacity) that meets it at the base.
- `logo-mark-simplified.svg`: single ink `sakura-red`, the nail beds solid and the nail shape behind them at 30% opacity, with no thin lines. Use below 48px.
- `logo-lockup-2000.png` and `logo-mark-1024.png`: transparent PNG exports for tools that cannot take SVG.
